Vanderbilt University Medical Center financial assistance: the complete 2026 guide

Vanderbilt University Medical Center (VUMC) is the academic medical center of Vanderbilt University, based in Nashville, with affiliated hospitals in Wilson County, Bedford, Tullahoma-Harton, and Clarksville. VUMC's financial assistance works on a sliding scale: the discount depends on your income and family size compared to the Federal Poverty Guidelines, and VUMC says it typically gives a decision within 30 days after it receives your application. This guide explains how the program works, what to bring, and how to apply.

2026 income limits

2026 income limits for Vanderbilt University Medical Center financial assistance

VUMC does not give a single flat income cutoff in its public summary. Instead, the discount you receive follows a sliding scale based on your adjusted gross income (AGI) and household size compared to the Federal Poverty Guidelines. The exact discount tiers are listed in the charity guidelines in the appendix to VUMC's Financial Assistance Policy. How to apply

How to apply for financial assistance at Vanderbilt University Medical Center

  1. Request an itemized bill first

    Before doing anything else, call VUMC customer service at (615) 936-0910 and request a fully itemized statement of every charge on your bill. Review it carefully, because billing errors happen. Correcting errors before applying means financial assistance is applied to the correct baseline amount.

  2. Read the Financial Assistance Policy

    Read VUMC's Financial Assistance Policy and plain-language summary at vanderbilthealth.com/information/financial-assistance. Find the charity guidelines in the policy appendix to see which income tier you fall into, and check the documentation requirements before you start.

  3. Complete the application

    You can apply online, call or visit customer service, or mail a request to 3841 Green Hills Village Dr., Ste. 200, Nashville, TN 37215. VUMC says a formal application may not always be required, and you may only need to answer a standard set of questions. If you are given a form, complete every section, since incomplete applications are a common cause of delays.

  4. Gather your documentation

    Collect the documents listed in the section below. Make copies of everything before submitting. If mailing, use certified mail with return receipt to create a dated delivery record.

  5. Submit and follow up

    Submit your application and supporting documents. Follow up by phone after 2 to 3 weeks to confirm receipt, get a case reference number, and ask about timing. Federal rules require nonprofit hospitals to accept applications for at least 240 days after the first post-discharge bill, but applying early is safer.

  6. Appeal if denied

    A denial is not always final. Ask for the specific reason in writing and submit a written appeal with additional or corrected documentation. Read our complete guide to appealing a denied charity care application for a step-by-step process.

Documents needed

What you may be asked for when applying for Vanderbilt University Medical Center financial assistance

VUMC bases eligibility on adjusted gross income for the current or prior year. Requirements can vary by situation, so confirm the exact list with customer service. Commonly requested items include:

  • The VUMC financial assistance application, or your answers to the standard screening questions
  • Most recent federal tax return showing Adjusted Gross Income (AGI)
  • Recent pay stubs if your income has changed from last year's return
  • Government benefit award letters (Social Security, disability, SNAP)
  • Bank statements if you are self-employed
  • If you have no income: a signed statement explaining your situation
What's unique about this hospital's policy

VUMC uses adjusted gross income (AGI): check your tax return, not just pay stubs

VUMC says qualification is based on the adjusted gross income of the patient (or the patient's household, if you file taxes jointly) for the current year or the prior year, compared to the Federal Poverty Guidelines. AGI is the figure on your tax return, and it can be lower than your gross wages, so use your 1040 figure rather than your pay stubs when estimating your tier.

Federal rules under IRS Section 501(r) set a floor for nonprofit hospitals like VUMC: they must have a written financial assistance policy, and eligible patients cannot be charged more than the amounts generally billed to insured patients for emergency or other medically necessary care. Cosmetic, elective, experimental, and transplant services may not qualify for a discount, so ask about your specific services.

FAQ

Vanderbilt financial assistance: common questions

Does Vanderbilt University Medical Center have a charity care or financial assistance program?

Yes. VUMC's Financial Assistance Policy offers discounts to patients with little or no insurance. The amount of assistance is based on income and family size compared to the Federal Poverty Guidelines, using a sliding scale, and financial hardship is also reviewed case by case.

How do I apply for financial assistance at Vanderbilt University Medical Center?

Apply online at vanderbilthealth.com/information/financial-assistance, call VUMC customer service at (615) 936-0910 (Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.), visit customer service in person, or mail a request to 3841 Green Hills Village Dr., Ste. 200, Nashville, TN 37215. VUMC says a formal application may not always be required; you may only need to answer a standard set of questions.

What is the income limit for Vanderbilt University Medical Center financial assistance in 2026?

VUMC bases the discount on a sliding scale using your adjusted gross income and household size compared to the Federal Poverty Guidelines. The exact income tiers are listed in the charity guidelines in the appendix to VUMC's Financial Assistance Policy, so confirm your tier there or with VUMC customer service before applying.
